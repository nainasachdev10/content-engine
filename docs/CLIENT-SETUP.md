# Getting started with your Content Engine

Everything happens in one place: your dashboard at **`<your dashboard link>`**. It works
on your computer and your phone. Nothing is ever published to YouTube until you approve it.

## First visit (about 5 minutes)

1. **Sign in** with the password you were given.
2. **Connect your accounts.** Paste your Anthropic, ElevenLabs and Replicate keys (each
   has a "Get a key" link). These are the accounts your videos are billed to; they stay
   on your engine and are never shown again.
3. **Describe your channel.** A couple of sentences about what it's about, who watches,
   and the tone you want. The engine writes its own style guide from this.
4. **Connect YouTube.** Sign in with the Google account that owns the channel and click
   *Allow*. This only lets the engine publish videos *after you approve them*.
5. **Turn on notifications.** Easiest on a phone: Telegram. In Telegram, message
   @BotFather, send `/newbot`, copy the token it gives you, paste it under Settings →
   Connections, then on Settings → Notifications open your bot, press *Start*, and click
   *Check connection*. (Email and browser push are also available.)

That's it. Press **Make a video** to start your first one, or turn on the schedule in
the channel's settings and videos will arrive on their own.

## Every day

You get a notification: *"Ready to review: …"*. Tap it.

- **Watch** the video. Click a line of the script to jump to that moment.
- **Approve & publish** — it goes up on your channel with the title, description, tags
  and thumbnail shown.
- **Request changes** — type what you'd tell an editor ("shorten the intro", "make the
  thumbnail bluer", "scene 4 should show a coral reef"). Only the affected parts are
  redone, and you can always go back to the previous version.
- **Reject** — archives it. Nothing is uploaded.

## Optional: an on-camera host

Prefer talking-head videos? In your channel's settings turn on **On-camera host** and
describe the presenter. A consistent host is created once and speaks the opening,
transitions and ending to camera; the rest of the video stays visuals. To use a real
person, ask your administrator to replace the host image with a photo.

## Optional: your Notion workspace

Connect Notion from the channel page to get a hub with your brand brief and four
databases: creator research, a content-ideas inbox, the video pipeline, and a run log.

- Add ideas to **Content Ideas** — they're scored every night and the strongest ones are
  made automatically.
- Add creators you admire to **Creator Teardowns** with *Status = To audit* — the engine
  analyses their hooks, titles and pacing and writes what to steal and what to avoid.
- Edit the **Hub** page to change your positioning, pillars or hard rules — the engine
  reads it before every video.

## Questions

- *How long does a video take?* 10–25 minutes depending on length.
- *Can I change a video after it's published?* Yes, on YouTube directly — the engine
  never touches published videos.
- *Can I stop the schedule?* Channel → Settings → untick *Make videos on a schedule*.
